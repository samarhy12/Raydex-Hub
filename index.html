What can I learn on Raydex Learn?

Raydex Learn at learn.raydexhub.com is an interactive learning platform covering web development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, Python programming, and machine learning. It is written for people starting from zero.

Who is Raphael Yeboah Wadie?

Agyarey Raphael Yeboah Wadie is an agricultural engineer (BSc, University of Ghana, Legon, 2024), software developer and police officer based in Koforidua, Eastern Region, Ghana. He founded Raydex Hub and plans to become a lecturer.

Are Raydex Transcriber and Background Remover free?

Yes. Both tools are free to use in your browser, with no installs and no watermarks.

Which languages does Raydex Transcriber support?

It supports 14 languages, including Twi, Hausa, Yoruba and Igbo, and can translate speech into an English transcript. It accepts audio and video files up to 500 MB.
